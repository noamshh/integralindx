About This Demo ▼

This is a personal research demonstration based on the recent paper "E-Gen: Leveraging E-Graphs to Improve Continuous Representations of Symbolic Expressions" by Zheng et al. (2025). The project implements E-Gen embeddings, a novel approach for creating semantic representations of mathematical expressions in a continuous vector space. The database contains 23,638 integral expressions sourced from Math StackExchange, serving as a playground to test how E-Gen embeddings capture mathematical similarity and return related expressions in the embedding space. The goal is to enable similarity search that goes beyond simple pattern matching to understand genuine mathematical structure and relationships.
